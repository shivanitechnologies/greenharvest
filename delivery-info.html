Our Eco Delivery Process

1

Morning Pre-Cooling

Harvested fruits and greens are placed in cool humidified holding rooms within 90 minutes of cutting to arrest enzymatic nutrient degradation.

2

Zero-Plastic Packing

Packed with plant-based moisture liners inside insulated recycled corrugated cartons with reusable non-toxic ice packs.

3

Direct Doorstep Handover

Delivered in our climate-regulated electric vans during your preferred morning or evening delivery slot with SMS tracking.

Our Produce Guarantee

100% No-Hassle Fresh Produce Replacement Policy

Because our harvest is 100% organic and free from artificial synthetic preservative waxes, living produce occasionally reacts to weather. If any fruit or vegetable in your order arrives damaged, bruised, or unsatisfactory, simply snap a picture and message our WhatsApp or email within 24 hours.

We will promptly issue a full refund to your original payment method or dispatch a replacement with your next morning basket. No return shipment required.

Delivery FAQs

What happens if I am not home when the delivery arrives? ▼

Our insulated thermal boxes are designed to preserve safe produce temperature for up to 4 hours on your shaded porch. Our driver will also call you or follow your specified drop instructions.

Do you recycle or take back the packing materials? ▼

Yes! Just leave your previous box and ice packs outside on your next delivery day. Our driver collects them for sanitization and reuse.
