Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about our delivery.

What happens if I'm not home when delivery arrives? ▼

Our insulated boxes keep produce cool and crisp for up to 4 hours on your porch. You can also leave delivery instructions (like a gate code or neighbor's house) during checkout.

How do I return the delivery boxes and ice packs? ▼

Simply leave your empty box and ice packs outside on your next delivery day. Our driver collects them for sanitization and reuse, helping keep packaging zero-waste.

Is there a minimum order amount? ▼

Yes, our minimum order value is $15. This allows us to keep delivery routes sustainable and minimize emissions per drop.

Can I change my delivery address or slot? ▼

Yes, you can update your delivery window or address up to 2 hours before your slot starts by contacting our support team or replying to your order SMS.
