Our Heritage & Roots • Pokusa for Health

Cultivating A Healthier Future From Pure Soil.

GreenHarvest began with a simple revelation: our food should nourish life, not deplete it. Witnessing how conventional supermarket chains prioritize artificial cosmetic perfection and extended shelf-life over natural enzymes, we founded GreenHarvest to reconnect city households with genuine regenerative farmers.

Today, our network spans over 45 partner micro-farms, delivering verified 100% certified organic fruits, crunchy root vegetables, wood-pressed cold oils, and pure forest honeys with zero chemical residues.

Est. 2018

Valley Hills Biodynamic Cooperative

Our Mission

To make certified chemical-free, nutrient-dense organic food effortlessly accessible to every kitchen table, while empowering generational family farmers through dignified wages, ethical trade, and regenerative soil restoration.

Our Vision

A future where industrial pesticide farming is replaced by vibrant, living biodiversity; where families know the exact soil where their morning apples and vegetables grew, and where clean food is the default foundation of human well-being.

Growing Through Integrity

Our Journey & Milestones

2018

The Seed is Planted

Started with 3 certified organic orchard growers in Valley Hills, delivering weekly baskets directly to 40 local families.

2020

Pantry & Traditional Oils Addition

Expanded to traditional wood-pressed Ghani oils, wild forest honey cooperatives, and unpolished heirloom grains.

2023

Cold Chain & Zero-Plastic Packaging

Achieved 100% biodegradable compostable packaging and introduced same-day temperature-controlled doorstep delivery.

Today

Serving 18,000+ Conscious Homes

Partnered with 45+ organic cooperatives across the region, upholding the strictest non-GMO and regenerative soil protocols.

Zero Compromise

How We Select Our Products

Criterion 1

Soil Microbiology Audit

Every farm must undergo multi-point tests verifying the absence of synthetic chemical fertilizers, organophosphates, and glyphosate.

Criterion 2

Ancestral Processing

Our oils are slowly pressed below 35°C in wooden Ghani vats; our honey is never boiled or micro-filtered; our spices remain non-irradiated.

Criterion 3

Transparent Batch Traceability

Every batch number corresponds to an authentic harvest date and farm origin that any customer can inspect on the product label.
